
CC2020 Competency C-002 Task 1: 

Evaluate Cloud Service Approaches for Enterprise Architecture

Description:
As part of an IT team, you are tasked with evaluating different cloud service approaches for a small retail business's enterprise architecture. Your goal is to provide a preliminary analysis to help the management team make an informed decision.

Instructions:
1. Preparation: Review the basics of enterprise architecture and virtual systems and services. Understand the key principles of IS management and leadership.
2. Analysis: Analyze the current enterprise architecture of the retail business and compare it against the organizational business model. Consider three different cloud service approaches (e.g., IaaS, PaaS, SaaS).
3. Evaluation: Evaluate the cost-benefit of each cloud service approach. Focus on aspects such as scalability, security, and cost-effectiveness.
4. Documentation: Write a short report summarizing your analysis and recommendations. Use clear and concise written communication.
5. Presentation: Prepare a brief oral presentation to share your findings with your team. Practice your oral communication skills to ensure clarity and professionalism.

Key Focus:
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 1: Analyze enterprise architecture.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 2: Analyze virtual systems and services.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 3: Evaluate IS management and leadership aspects.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 4: Analyze research and self-starter learning.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 5: Understand oral communication and presentation.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 6: Apply written communication skills.

- Dispositions: Proactive, Self-directed, Professional.

Example Solution:
Create a document that includes a summary of the current architecture, an analysis of three cloud service approaches, cost-benefit evaluations, and a recommendation. Additionally, prepare a PowerPoint presentation to present your findings.








CC2020 Competency C-002 Task 2: 
Cost-Benefit Analysis of Cloud Services for Enterprise Architecture

Description:
You are working as a junior consultant for a mid-sized manufacturing company. Your task is to conduct a cost-benefit analysis of implementing cloud services within the company’s enterprise architecture.

Instructions:
1. Preparation: Study the fundamentals of enterprise architecture and virtual systems and services. Gain insights into effective written and oral communication techniques.
2. Research: Research three cloud service providers (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and their offerings. Focus on their relevance to the company’s needs.
3. Analysis: Analyze how each cloud service aligns with the company's business model and goals. Pay attention to features, benefits, and potential drawbacks.
4. Cost-Benefit Evaluation: Perform a cost-benefit analysis for each service provider. Include factors such as initial setup costs, ongoing maintenance costs, potential savings, and long-term benefits.
5. Report: Document your findings in a detailed report. Ensure the report is professionally written and includes all necessary data and conclusions.
6. Presentation: Prepare to present your findings to the company’s management team. Focus on being proactive in your presentation approach, ensuring you cover all key points effectively.

Key Focus:
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 1: Analyze enterprise architecture.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 2: Analyze virtual systems and services.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 3: Evaluate IS management and leadership aspects.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 4: Analyze research and self-starter learning.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 5: Understand oral communication and presentation.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 6: Apply written communication skills.

- Dispositions: Proactive, Self-directed, Professional.

Example Solution:
Draft a comprehensive report detailing your research, analysis, and recommendations for each cloud service provider. Additionally, create a presentation to summarize your findings and prepare to discuss them with the management team.

These tasks are designed to ensure that learners apply their knowledge and skills in a practical, low-difficulty setting, while also embodying the key dispositions of being proactive, self-directed, and professional.
