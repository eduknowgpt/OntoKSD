##### TASK04

TASK04 a LearningTask
TASK04 hasAcquitionContext contextEduknow
Title: The Return of the Farmer Robot
Description: The task challenges students to design a navigation module that allows the robot to autonomously return to its initial location after issuing food delivery alerts. The module must be implemented using computational models and validated through simulation. This scenario is rooted in a real-world agricultural context and emphasizes the application of **formal methods**, including **automata theory** and **context-free grammars**, to address autonomous navigation problems.
Authors: Luiz Gavaza,  Lais Salvador,  Daniel Cason,  Roberta Oliveira, Jéssica Santana, Otávio Neto
