###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#CompetenceElement
ontoksd:CompetenceElement rdf:type owl:Class ;
                          owl:disjointWith ontoksd:KnowledgeSkill ;
                          <http://purl.org/dc/elements/1.1/description> "Competence elements correspond to personal characteristics that constitute the underlying structure of a competence, as described by Miranda et al. (2018)."@en ;
                          dcterms:bibliographicCitation "Cited as Competency in Miranda, S. et al. (2018). An Ontology-Based Model for Competence Management. Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing."@en ;
                          rdfs:comment """A core constituent of a Competence, representing a single conceptual component that contributes to the overall ability to perform a task.

In OntoKSD, CompetenceElement is an abstract class specialized into three mutually exclusive categories:

Knowledge: Conceptual and factual information relevant to the competence domain.

Skill: The ability to apply knowledge to perform actions or solve problems.

Disposition: Attitudes, values, and behavioral tendencies that influence the performance of the competence.

CompetenceElements are static entities and form the structural building blocks for defining Competences."""@en ;
                          rdfs:label "CompetenceElement"@en .

###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#Disposition
ontoksd:Disposition rdf:type owl:Class ;
                    rdfs:subClassOf ontoksd:CompetenceElement ;
                    rdfs:comment """A relatively stable tendency or inclination to act or respond in certain ways in specific contexts, reflecting values, motivations, and personal attributes that influence the application of knowledge and skills.

In OntoKSD, Disposition captures the affective and behavioral dimension of competence, which shapes how an individual approaches tasks and interacts with others.

Dispositions are not merely internal attitudes but have observable manifestations that can be linked to professional and social effectiveness."""@en .

###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#Competence
ontoksd:Competence rdf:type owl:Class ;
                   rdfs:comment """A formal, ontological description of an integrated set of knowledge, skills, and dispositions that enables an individual to perform a task or fulfill a role effectively in a given domain.

In OntoKSD, a Competence is a static, reusable conceptual entity that defines what is expected for successful performance, independently of any specific individual or assessment event. It serves as a reference model for competency-based curriculum design, instructional alignment, and performance evaluation."""@en ;
                   rdfs:label "Competence"@en .
