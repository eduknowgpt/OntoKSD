###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#Evidence

                 rdfs:subClassOf [ rdf:type owl:Restriction ;

In OntoKSD, Evidence serves as the bridge between competence descriptions and real-world performance, capturing measurable manifestations of knowledge, skills, and dispositions.

Each piece of evidence is temporally bound (associated with a date or period), may be linked to a specific EvidenceType, and is collected in connection with an instructional activity, professional task, or other relevant context.

Evidence can originate from both formal and informal settings and is essential for supporting assessment, certification, and learning analytics."""@en ;
                 rdfs:label "Evidence"@en .


###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#EvidenceType
ontoksd:EvidenceType rdf:type owl:Class ;
                     rdfs:comment """A classification of evidence based on its method of collection or the nature of the activity that produced it.
In OntoKSD, EvidenceType enables semantic differentiation between various forms of proof, supporting more precise competency evaluation and reasoning.
Typical examples include:

SelfAssessment – Evidence produced by an individual’s own evaluation of their competence.

PeerAssessment – Evidence based on evaluation by peers or colleagues.

TeacherAssessment – Evidence provided by an instructor or mentor.

ProjectArtifact – Deliverables or products generated as part of a project or task.

PerformanceObservation – Direct observation of task performance in real or simulated environments.

WrittenExam – Performance on a written test assessing specific competencies.

Presentation – Oral or multimedia presentation demonstrating competence.

PortfolioEvidence – A curated collection of works or artifacts demonstrating sustained competence over time."""@en .
