###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#LearningRole
ontoksd:LearningRole rdf:type owl:Class ;
                     rdfs:comment """A LearningRole is an educational role assumed by a learner within a course, discipline, or instructional activity. It contextualizes the learner’s participation by specifying the responsibilities, expected behaviors, and competencies associated with that role.

Core Idea

It represents how a learner is positioned within a specific instructional context (e.g., student in Algorithms, group leader in a project, teaching assistant in a course).

It provides semantic links between roles and competencies, enabling the specification of which competencies are required, expected, or likely to be developed when acting in a given role.

It allows for the contextual interpretation of performance evaluations, since competence evidence is relative to the role played by the learner at the time."""@en .

###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#CompetenceDetail
ontoksd:CompetenceDetail rdf:type owl:Class ;
                         rdfs:comment """A contextualized, individual-specific record that links a Competence to concrete proof of its acquisition or demonstration.

In OntoKSD, CompetenceDetail captures measurable data derived from assessment activities, performance records, or verifiable artifacts.
It includes contextual metadata such as acquisition date, assessment method, and environment in which the competence was demonstrated.

Unlike Competence, which is a static reference model, CompetenceDetail is dynamic and time-dependent, reflecting the actual performance of an individual at a given moment.

An evidential record of a person’s demonstrated competence in a context; bearer of ProficiencyLevel qualities.\"@en ."""@en .
