###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#AcquisitionContext
ontoksd:AcquisitionContext rdf:type owl:Class ;
                           rdfs:subClassOf ontoksd:Context ;
                           rdfs:label "Acquisition Context"@en ;
                           skos:definition """A specific contextual setting in which an instructional entity (e.g., course, learning task, training module) is delivered and within which competence acquisition can be observed, facilitated, or evaluated.
In OntoKSD, AcquisitionContext represents the instructional and environmental conditions under which learners engage in activities designed to develop specific competencies. It combines situational factors (e.g., learning environment, resources, constraints) with pedagogical arrangements (e.g., teaching methods, collaborative structures) that influence the learning process and the demonstration of competence.

Unlike the broader Context, which may describe any situation in which a competence is applied or demonstrated, AcquisitionContext is specifically tied to learning and assessment processes. It situates instructional entities in time and space, providing the background against which evidence of competence acquisition can be generated.

Examples:

A laboratory session in a computer science course where students collaboratively design and test algorithms.

An online simulation platform used for training project management skills under time-constrained scenarios.

A capstone project in an engineering program involving interdisciplinary teamwork and industry mentorship.

Ontologically, AcquisitionContext can be modeled as a specialized form of Context focused on the instructional phase of competence development, typically associated with agents (learners, instructors), tasks, and specific temporal boundaries."""@en .

###  http://purl.org/ontoksd#InstructionalEntity
ontoksd:InstructionalEntity rdf:type owl:Class ;
                            rdfs:comment """An InstructionalEntity is an abstract educational artifact—such as a course component, learning activity, resource, or assessment—designed to enable the acquisition, reinforcement, or evaluation of competencies in a specific context.

InstructionalEntity encompasses any deliberately created learning experience or educational object, regardless of format, that is linked to targeted competencies either as learning goals or prerequisite requirements. It also serves as a source of competence evidence, as the performance of learners within the entity’s scope can be evaluated to determine the extent of competence acquisition.

Definition:
An InstructionalEntity is any structured educational activity, resource, or assessment designed with the explicit purpose of supporting, validating, or evidencing one or more competencies in a specific instructional and contextual setting.

Examples include:
– Formal learning units: courses, modules, workshops, seminars.
– Practical or performance-based activities: projects, simulations, problem-based learning scenarios, internships.
– Assessments and evaluation tools: written exams, presentations, portfolios, skill demonstrations.
– Digital learning resources: e-learning modules, interactive simulations, multimedia learning objects.

Ontological notes:

InstructionalEntity is an abstract class that can be specialized according to different educational domains, such as formal education, corporate training, or informal learning.

It connects to Competence instances via explicit relations such as targetsCompetence (competencies intended for development) and requiresCompetence (prerequisite competencies).

It is anchored to an AcquisitionContext, which defines the environmental and pedagogical conditions under which it is implemented.

Evidence generated from learner engagement in an InstructionalEntity is represented through Evidence and EvidenceType, supporting competency assessment and learner profiling."""@en ;
                            rdfs:label "Instructional Entity"@en .
