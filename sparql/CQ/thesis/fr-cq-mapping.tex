CQ32, CQ39, CQ40, and CQ42 retain supplementary scope. The implemented
CQ32a retrieves an existing attribution and supplies no synthesis
procedure. CQ39a, CQ40b, and CQ42b repeat the same comparison policy;
their equivalence does not increase independent requirement coverage.
Requirement conclusions must distinguish support, partial support,
lack of support, and absence of evaluation.
