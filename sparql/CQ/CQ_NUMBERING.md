The post-qualification revision retains families CQ1–CQ42. A suffix identifies an operational view within a family; it is not a new ordinal question. There are 58 executable files, not 58 independent conceptual questions. No files are renamed.

CQ14–CQ17 now have explicit implementations. CQ20c1/20c2 are focused views under CQ20; the nested suffixes retain prior references. CQ32a is a retrieval alias and does not implement the original synthesis ambition.
