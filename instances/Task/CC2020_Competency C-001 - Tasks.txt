
CC2020  Competency C-001 Task 1: 

Identify and Document System Requirements

Description:
As a member of a software development team, you are assigned to identify and document system requirements for a new library management system. You will conduct a requirements elicitation session with stakeholders to gather the necessary information.

Instructions:
1. Preparation: Review the basics of requirements analysis and specifications, and understand the key components of written communication in technical contexts.
2. Elicitation Session: Participate in a work session with stakeholders (e.g., librarians, library staff, IT personnel) to gather requirements using a known requirements elicitation technique such as interviews or focus groups.
3. Documentation: Document the identified requirements clearly and concisely. Ensure the document includes all necessary specifications and is understandable to all team members and stakeholders.
4. Collaboration: Work collaboratively with your team, actively listen to stakeholder input, and ensure all viewpoints are considered.

Key Focus:
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 1: Apply requirements analysis and specifications.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 2: Apply written communication skills.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 3: Apply systems analysis and design principles.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 4: Analyze collaboration and teamwork dynamics.

- Dispositions: Purpose-driven, Responsible, Collaborative.

Example Solution:
Prepare a requirements document that includes functional and non-functional requirements, user stories, and any constraints or assumptions.








CC2020 Competency C-001 Task 2: 

Facilitate a Requirements Workshop

Description:
You are responsible for facilitating a requirements workshop for a new e-commerce website project. Your goal is to gather detailed system requirements by engaging with various stakeholders, including marketing, sales, and IT departments.

Instructions:
1. Preparation: Study the principles of systems analysis and design, and review techniques for effective written communication. Understand the facilitative skills needed to guide a workshop.
2. Workshop Facilitation: Lead a requirements workshop where stakeholders present their needs and expectations. Use techniques such as brainstorming or scenario analysis to elicit detailed requirements.
3. Documentation: Write a comprehensive report documenting the gathered requirements, ensuring clarity and precision. Include diagrams or models to support the written text.
4. Team Dynamics: Coordinate with your team to ensure all contributions are integrated into the final document. Foster a collaborative environment where all team members feel valued and responsible for the outcome.

Key Focus:
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 1: Apply requirements analysis and specifications.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 2: Apply written communication skills.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 3: Apply systems analysis and design principles.
- Knowledge-Skill Pair 4: Analyze collaboration and teamwork dynamics.

- Dispositions: Purpose-driven, Responsible, Collaborative.

Example Solution:
Create a detailed report that includes a summary of the workshop, a list of all identified requirements, and any relevant diagrams or models to illustrate the system design.

These tasks are designed to ensure that learners apply their knowledge and skills in a practical, low-difficulty setting, while also embodying the key dispositions of being purpose-driven, responsible, and collaborative.
