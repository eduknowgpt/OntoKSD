# OntoKSD in concrete cases

These cases explain what the represented relationships make available for examination. Historical encodings, retrospective judgments, fictional examples, and technical fixtures have different evidential roles.

## Reuse one specification across activities

**Question:** Can a technical-reporting specification remain the same while its instructional contribution differs?

C05, *Write a Technical Report*, is associated with Task201 and Task202 through separate activations. The specification identifies the expected performance; the records qualify its local use.

- [Cycle 1 reference specifications](../competenceInstances/CSP/cycle-01/README.md).
- [Cycle 2 task encoding](../competenceInstances/CSP/cycle-02/README.md).
- [CQ35–CQ36 and other views](../sparql/CQ/CQ_CATALOG.md).

The benefit is an inspectable distinction between definition and use. The adequacy of an association still depends on the task and its justification.

## Trace a recorded judgment to its documentary basis

**Question:** Which participation, evidence, and criteria support one individual interpretation?

The [Task202 retrospective supplement](../competenceInstances/CSP/cycle-02/assessment-records/task202/retrospective-c05/README.md) represents a C05 judgment adopted by the thesis author about a learner's documented writing contribution to a coauthored report. It identifies participation, occasion, evidence, two applied criteria, and three supporting documents.

The [SELECT](../competenceInstances/CSP/cycle-02/assessment-records/task202/retrospective-c05/queries/01-fr11-retrospective-trace.rq) retrieves six combinations: two criteria by three documents. These are rows about **one judgment**, not six independent assessments. No proficiency level was assigned.

This makes the documentary basis inspectable. The adequacy of individual attribution remains an educational question: tutors assessed the group report, and the contribution was team-reported. Original documents retain their access conditions.

## Describe composition and curricular correspondence

**Question:** How can a catalog preserve components and explain a local contribution to a curricular expectation?

The [Cycle 4 dataset](../competenceInstances/CSP/cycle-04/README.md) represents introductory-programming specifications, including CM10 and CM18 compositions and qualified BNCC Computação correspondences. Component membership, coordinated performance, direct instructional association, and external correspondence are separately recorded.

A qualified correspondence preserves endpoints and explanation. It does not replace a local definition with an external statement or establish complete curricular coverage.

## Interpret proficiency under an identified scheme

The [fictional proficiency example](../proficiency%20level/README.md) separates a decimal score, category intervals, an interpretation scheme, and an expected category. Queries apply a stated same-scheme policy. Missing and below-expectation judgments produce different retrieval conditions.

This is an analysis of records under that policy, not a diagnosis, passing rule, or measure of learner progression.
